ZeroFrik Website Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the ZeroFrik company website located at https://zerofrik.com. It describes how ZeroFrik collects, uses, and safeguards information when visitors interact with this website.

Distinction: 1Tap Android Application

Please note that this policy does not cover the 1Tap Android mobile app. 1Tap is an on-device personal finance application that processes financial SMS data locally on the user's phone without transmitting expense data to ZeroFrik servers. 1Tap maintains its own dedicated privacy policy, accessible at 1tap.zerofrik.com/#privacy.

2. Information Submitted Voluntarily Through Forms

ZeroFrik collects personal information that you provide voluntarily when submitting an inquiry through our contact or project consultation forms:

  • Name: Provided in project enquiry forms to identify the prospective client or representative.
  • Email Address: Used as our direct contact point to respond to your inquiry or consultation request.
  • Company Name: Provided voluntarily to understand your business organization and context.
  • Selected Service: Category selection (e.g., E-commerce Development, Custom Plugins & Apps, Integrations, Payments, Performance, or General Enquiry).
  • Message & Project Details: Information, requirements, or problem descriptions you share in the text field.

3. How We Use Submitted Information

We process the information you submit through this website exclusively for clear and limited business purposes:

  • To review, evaluate, and respond to your message or project enquiry.
  • To communicate regarding proposed services, technical specifications, or consultation.
  • To manage and improve our business relationship with clients and prospective partners.

4. Google Analytics & Website Usage Information

ZeroFrik uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may process information such as browser/device information, page interactions, first-party identifiers/cookies, and coarse location information. Google states that raw IP addresses are not logged or stored in Google Analytics.

We do not send personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, or form message contents) to Google Analytics. Analytics metrics are evaluated on an aggregate level to assess user interest, identify navigation issues, and improve website performance.

You can manage or disable cookie preferences directly through your web browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted third-party service providers to deliver and operate our website:

  • Google Apps Script / Workspace: When you submit a form, data is transmitted to an automated Google Apps Script endpoint used to deliver and store inquiry notifications for our team.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): For website performance and traffic analytics, as detailed in Section 4.
  • Content Delivery & Web Hosting: Third-party hosting infrastructure that serves static assets and website files.

6. Data Sharing, Selling & Disclosure

ZeroFrik does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks. We only share information with third-party service providers as necessary to facilitate communication, host the website, and deliver requested services as outlined above. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or formal legal process.

7. Data Retention & Security

We retain form submission records only for as long as necessary to respond to your requests, provide software development services, or fulfill administrative and business requirements. While no method of digital transmission or storage is guaranteed 100% impenetrable, we employ reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect submitted information against unauthorized access or disclosure.
