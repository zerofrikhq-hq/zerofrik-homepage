Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OneTap need my bank login?

No. OneTap does not need your bank username, password, or bank login.

How does automatic tracking work?

OneTap reads eligible bank transaction SMS on your Android phone and extracts supported transaction details locally.

Is my financial data uploaded?

OneTap is designed to keep transaction data on your device and does not require a cloud account.

Does OneTap require SMS permission?

Automatic SMS tracking requires SMS read permission. You can still use manual transaction entry without SMS access.

Can I edit a transaction?

Yes. You can edit transaction details and categories. OneTap can remember eligible corrections for future matching transactions.

Does OneTap identify every merchant?

Merchant identification depends on the information available in the transaction message. When a merchant is unavailable or uncertain, you can correct the transaction manually.

Will transfers be counted as spending?

Money Out currently includes confirmed outgoing transactions, including transfers. Transfer-specific classification may be added in a future version.

Does OneTap work offline?

OneTap is designed around local processing and does not require a cloud account for its core transaction-tracking experience.
