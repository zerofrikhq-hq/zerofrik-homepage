Foundations

What we believe

PRINCIPLE 01

Real problems first

Start with problems people actually experience. Avoid solutions manufactured in isolation looking for an arbitrary market fit.

PRINCIPLE 02

Simple beats complicated

Build the smallest useful solution before adding complexity. Elegance is found in removal, not cumulative feature bloat.

PRINCIPLE 03

Users decide

Real usage matters more than assumptions. Unsolicited user reliance is the only objective validator of software utility.

PRINCIPLE 04

Earn the right to grow

Products earn more investment by proving that they create genuine value. We scale operations proportional to proven retention.
